# Product vision — draft v0.1

**Working name**: TikiTaka. Audience: adults 18+.

## Positioning
A social-dating app where connection, playful interaction, and friendships coexist without stigma.

## Experiences
- **Day**: personal discovery, compatibility, honest intentions, calm interactions.
- **Night**: opt-in social planning, groups, and playful games (truth/dare, bottle, questions) with consent and safety controls.
- **Jugador**: participates in discovery, conversations and plans.
- **Cupido**: optionally suggests introductions among consenting people. No impersonation, unilateral contact or exposing private profiles.

## Experience principles
- User controls mode at all times; optional suggested transition ~21:00 local time.
- Never auto-publish location, drinking status, sexual preferences, or night activity.
- Group invites and game participation require opt-in; explicit leave/block/report controls.
- Prioritize quality, accessibility, clear boundaries and moderation over endless swipes.
- Rewards for positive contributions; no pay-to-pressure or coercive mechanics.

## Non-goals for initial MVP
Real-time nightlife location maps, payments, AI matching, gamified currency, video calls, unmoderated anonymous chat.

## Decisions pending
Final brand and domain legal clearance; privacy/age-assurance model; matching rules; moderation process; internationalization; launch geography.
